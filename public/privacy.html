Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use Blok Yarışı (the "app", the "game"), how it's used, and what rights you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Game profile: a random identifier tied to your device (profile ID), your best score, your Blok Coin balance, and the cosmetics you own.
  • Account information (if you sign up): the username you choose and a one-way (irreversible) hashed version of your password. Your actual password is never stored.
  • Friends list: the usernames of friends you add or who add you.
  • Purchase information: if you buy a Blok Coin package, the transaction is confirmed through the payment provider (Stripe, or the Apple/Google app store); your card details never reach us and are handled directly by the payment provider.
  • Technical data: IP address and standard server logs, kept briefly and only for security and debugging purposes.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

You can earn extra Blok Coin by watching rewarded ads. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and some device information to show and measure ads. This data is subject to Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Keeping your game progress (score, coins, cosmetics, friends) in sync across devices
  • Preventing cheating/abuse (all score and coin calculations happen on the server)
  • Verifying purchases
  • Fixing technical issues

Other than our advertising and payment providers, we never sell or rent your information to any third party.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below. Your request will be honored within a reasonable time.

5. Children's Privacy

Blok Yarışı does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we've collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

6. Security

Passwords are stored irreversibly (salted and hashed). All game logic and coin calculations happen on the server rather than the client, which helps protect your account data from tampering.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date on this page will change whenever we make a meaningful update.
